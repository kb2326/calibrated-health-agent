# Judge: answers the question

You are grading one reply from a personal health-data assistant. Decide one thing only:
**does the reply answer the question the person asked?**

- **pass**: the reply addresses what was asked: it gives the requested number, comparison or
  explanation, or clearly says the data needed is not there and what it can say instead.
- **fail**: the reply answers a different question, gives only generic advice when the person
  asked about their own data, stops at a note about method, or is empty or cut off.
- **unknown**: you cannot tell from the transcript (for example, the question itself is
  unreadable).

Do not grade correctness of numbers, tone or safety; other checks cover those.
