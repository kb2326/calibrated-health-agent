# Forecasting protocol

This page explains how the forecast ledger works, in plain words. The binding version is the
pre-registered plan, [`docs/ledger/analysis-plan.md`](../ledger/analysis-plan.md). It was
fixed on 2026-10-23 before the first locked forecast. Its sha256 is the first entry of the
ledger's hash chain, and the chain's daily hashes are published at
https://github.com/kb2326/cha-ledger-log. Where this page and the plan differ, the plan wins.
Nothing here changes it.

## What is predicted

Each morning, several forecasters bet on the owner's evening self-ratings for that day:
mood, energy and soreness, each an integer 1–10. A bet is a full probability
distribution, not a single number.

## Why it can be trusted

- **Locked before the outcome.** A day's bets are locked at the first run after the night's
  sleep data arrives, or by noon, and are hashed into a chain. A journal entry submitted
  before the lock voids the day.
- **Blind.** The owner cannot see unsettled bets. The agent's SQL refuses the ledger tables
  outright, and the only window into results is the aggregate scoreboard.
- **Fixed inputs.** Every forecaster gets the same feature snapshot: wearables through the
  morning's sleep and journal outcomes through yesterday, nothing later.
- **No filling in.** A forecaster that cannot bet abstains with a reason. That day is left
  out of its comparisons, never imputed.

## The forecasters

1. climatology, a smoothed history of all outcomes;
2. persistence, the latest outcome with the usual day-to-day spread;
3. seasonal naive, the same weekday last week;
4. statistical, a regression on the open readiness score;
5. TimesFM through BigQuery;
6. Gemini on Vertex AI, given the snapshot;
7. Gemini plus the investigator's out-of-sample-tested hypotheses (from Phase 3).

## How it is scored

- **Ranked probability score (RPS)** is primary, and the log score is secondary. Skill
  against a reference = 1 − mean RPS / reference mean RPS, over days both scored. Positive
  means better than the reference.
- **Primary endpoint:** energy, forecaster 6 against persistence. The 95% interval comes from
  a paired moving-block bootstrap (block 7, 2000 resamples, seed 20261023). It is reported
  only from 10 paired days.
- **Secondary:** mood and soreness, every forecaster against climatology and persistence,
  log scores, calibration (randomized PIT, 5 bins), and forecaster 7 against forecaster 6.

## What the results page adds

[`results.md`](results.md) is generated by `cha-ledger report`. These additions describe the
plan's numbers; they do not replace any of them:

- **Diebold–Mariano with the Harvey–Leybourne–Newbold correction** on the daily RPS
  difference, a standard test for "is one forecaster's loss lower", suited to short series.
- **Reliability with a consistency band.** For each PIT bin, the grey band shows where a
  perfectly calibrated forecaster's count would fall 95% of the time. Seeded, so the band is
  the same on every run.
- **Sharpness:** the mean spread of each forecaster's distributions.

The page carries aggregates only, rounded to 2 decimals, with no dates. A row with fewer than
10 days shows its n and "not yet" instead of numbers, because a mean over a handful of days
is close to a daily value.

## Honesty rules (from the plan)

Results are published as they are, including forecasters that lose to persistence. The real
number of scored days is next to every number. Only live locked forecasts count; any backtest
would appear separately and labelled.
