Pick a look for the web app

Three directions for the same screen, all on synthetic data: you ask "How did I sleep this week?", the agent shows its steps, the answer streams in with a sleep chart, and a receipt is open on the right. Each mockup has its own dark and light mode. Tell me A, B or C (or a mix, like "A's layout with C's colours").

All numbers are synthetic. Average 409 minutes asleep a night = 6 h 49 min; best Tue 458 min (7 h 38 min); shortest Sat 352 min (5 h 52 min). The chart is drawn to scale from those seven values.
